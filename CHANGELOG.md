# Changelog

All notable changes to the Library lending service are recorded here.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `GET /loans/overdue` lists overdue loans, most overdue first, with the fee owed if returned today.

## [1.0.0]

### Added

- Book catalog, member lookup, borrowing, and returns.
- Loan policy: per-membership loan limits and copy availability.
- Due dates that skip closed days, and late fees under board policy LIB-12.
